# MatterCatalog

Use the MatterCatalog tools when someone asks whether a smart home device is Matter certified, which Matter devices fit their platform (Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings) or network (Thread, Wi-Fi), how two Matter products compare, or where a Matter product is sold in the US.

- check_matter_certification: certificate IDs and dates from MatterCatalog's read of the CSA Distributed Compliance Ledger.
- find_matter_devices: catalog products by category, platform, network and brand.
- where_to_buy: the stores a product's MatterCatalog page links (store names only).
- compare: two products side by side.
- catalog_stats: dated certification and catalog figures.

Every answer is dated and links the mattercatalog.com page; prices and store buttons are on that page.
